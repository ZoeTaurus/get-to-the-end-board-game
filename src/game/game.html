How to Play

Pieces

Person-shaped piece:

  • Can move back, forth, and sideways
  • Can only eat opponent's pieces diagonally

Circle-shaped piece:

  • Can move in any direction
  • Can eat in any direction
  • Can only eat 2 pieces before getting full

How to Win

  • Eat all opponent's pieces, OR
  • Get to the other side of the board
